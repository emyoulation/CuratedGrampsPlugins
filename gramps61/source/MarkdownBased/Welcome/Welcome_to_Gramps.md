## ![](gramps:icon:gramps-view:32) Gramps for genealogical research
***Cut to the chase:*** discover how to [start entering data](#enter-your-first-family) or [import data](#importing-a-family-tree) from another program.

**Gramps** is a powerful, free, and open-source software application for genealogical research. Created by and for family historians in 2001, it combines professional-grade tools with a flexible design that lets you record, analyze, and explore your family tree your own way. Unlike many genealogy programs, Gramps does not lock you into a single “best practice” or trap your data in a proprietary format. Your research remains fully portable and accessible, ensuring that your family history can be preserved and shared across generations.

Because **Gramps** is *Free and Open Source Software*, you are encouraged to adapt it to suit your own needs. If you make improvements, we invite you to share them so the entire community can benefit. You are also free to copy and distribute Gramps without restrictions, and with licensing that never expires, you can trust that your research will remain your own — today and in the future.

Explore the [reference resources available for Gramps](https://gramps.discourse.group/t/about-the-user-manual-category/2585). ### Who makes Gramps?
Gramps is created by genealogists for genealogists, organized in the Gramps Project by the ***Gramps Genealogy Foundation***, a non-profit organization.

It is developed and maintained by a worldwide team of volunteers whose goal is to make Gramps powerful, yet easy to use. (You have a standing invitation to join as one of those volunteers!)

There is an active community of user volunteers available on the mailing lists and the [**Gramps Discourse**](https://discourse.gramps-project.org) forum to answer questions and share ideas and techniques.

### Getting Started
The first time Gramps is started, most of the Views will be blank. There will be very few menu or toolbar options. Gramps needs a Tree with People before more menu and toolbar options appear.

This is because options are offered on a contextual basis. (If an option does not apply to what is displayed or selected, that option is not shown.) The basic context of a Family Tree is needed for any activity to happen. Family Trees are your "blank document" or "new project" workspaces.

To create a new Family Tree (sometimes called a 'database'), select the "Family Trees" menu, pick the "Manage Family Trees..." option, press the "New" button, and name your Family Tree. Then click the "Load Family Tree" button to make the selected tree active and ready to accept genealogical research data.

If you are just exploring, scroll down to the [Example Database](#example-database) section to learn about importing the example Tree that is included with Gramps. Make any "beginner mistakes" there instead of in your research Tree. After a bit of exploring, you will be ready to begin entering your first family or importing a genealogy file. For one strategy for filling in the Tree, please read the information at the links below.
* [Start with Genealogy and Gramps](https://gramps-project.org/wiki/index.php?title=Start_with_Genealogy)
* [Discourse forum list of Gramps tutorial videos](https://gramps.discourse.group/t/tutorial-videos/126)

### Enter your first Family
Switch to the "Relationships" view and, from the "Add" menu, select "Family" to bring up the "Edit Family" window. Here you can click the ![](gramps:icon:gtk-add:24)"plus" icon beside "Father/partner1" or "Mother/partner2" to begin entering that person. Start with just the basic Name information for one person. (We will come back to add Birth and Death data later.) Clicking the "OK" button stores the record, creating your first Person. You can add an immediate relative in one of the other spots, or click the "OK" button to store that first family.
![Adding a Father/partner1 to a new Family](media/AddFamily_en.png)

This establishes a starting point for your tree. With the context of this first person and family, all of the menu options and toolbar icon functions become available. Spend some time moving your mouse over the icons. As your cursor passes over an icon, a hint message appears telling you the function of that icon. The same hint system is useful for exploring any of the edit windows. Moving the mouse cursor over an item tells you what that control does.

You can now expand families by adding parents, a spouse and children. Under the tabs of the lower section of the Edit windows, the ![](gramps:icon:gtk-add:24)"plus" icon under the Events tab allows adding landmark life occasions (with dates and places) to People and Families. Under the other tabs, you can add Sources, Citations, Notes and other types of information to provide documentation for your entries.

As you start using Gramps, you will find that information can be entered from all the various Views. There are multiple ways of doing most activities in Gramps. This flexibility lets you choose the way that fits your work style.
* [Entering and editing data (a brief overview)](https://gramps-project.org/wiki/index.php?title=Gramps_6.0_Wiki_Manual_-_Entering_and_editing_data:_brief)

### Importing a Family Tree
To import a Family Tree from another program, first export a GEDCOM (or other data exchange format) file from your previous program.

Create a new, blank Gramps database (Tree) as described in the [Getting Started](#getting-started) section above. Then use the "Import..." option under the "Family Trees" menu to import the GEDCOM data.
* [Import from another genealogy program](https://gramps-project.org/wiki/index.php?title=Import_from_another_genealogy_program)

### ![](gramps:icon:gramps-gramplet:64) Dashboard View
You are currently reading from the "Dashboard" view, where you can add your own gramplets. You can also add gramplets to any view by adding a sidebar and/or bottombar, and right-clicking to the right of the tabs.

![](gramps:icon:gramps-config:24) Choosing "Configure..." from the View menu (or clicking its icon in the toolbar) opens the "Gramplet Layout" tab. This lets you divide the dashboard into more or fewer columns.

![](gramps:icon:document-properties:24) You can also drag the Properties button to reposition the gramplet within this dashboard, or click it to detach the gramplet so that it floats above Gramps or can be placed on a second monitor.

While the Dashboard view is about using Gramps more efficiently, the other view categories support data entry and help you understand how your data interconnects.

### Addons and "Gramplets"
![Gramps Addon Plugins Guide](media/GrampsAddons.png)
There are hundreds of Addons (plugins) available to assist you in data entry and in visualizing your family tree. These addons provide reports, filter rules, View modes, "Gramplets", and more.

Many tools are already distributed with the basic installation of Gramps. Many more are freely available to download and install.
* [List of Addons and "Gramplets"](https://gramps-project.org/wiki/index.php?title=6.0_Addons)

### Example Database
Want to see Gramps in use? There is a demonstration family tree database that was used in the screen captures illustrating the documentation. You can create a blank tree and then import that Example database. This allows you to follow the examples while seeing exactly the same output. It also lets you experiment freely without worrying about damaging your own data. (You can always delete the example tree and re-import it to get back to the data shown in the manual.)

Create a new Family Tree as described in the [Getting Started](#getting-started) section above. (A good name for that Family Tree would be “EXAMPLE”.)

Then import the example.gramps archive file. (The webpage below describes what is in that archive and where the file is located on the different Operating Systems (OSes) that run Gramps. It also describes where the file is available on the web.)
* [Example.gramps](https://gramps-project.org/wiki/index.php?title=Example.gramps)
